Maxim Galkin attends a wedding in Baku

16 November 20261 min read👁 viewsShowband Group
Weddings

Well-known Russian showman Maxim Galkin reportedly attended one of the weddings held in Baku. Footage from the celebration sparked wide discussion on social media.

Eye-catching shots from a Baku wedding

News of the famous showman’s visit to our capital spread quickly on social media. Maxim Galkin reportedly attended a private celebration in Baku. Well-known host Leyla Guliyeva was the first to share photos from the event with her followers, and they quickly drew attention.

Famous faces together

Leyla Guliyeva’s photos show not only the Russian showman but also Honoured Artist of Azerbaijan Abbas Bagirov. The pictures capture interesting moments of artists getting together, and followers left a variety of comments. In her caption, Leyla Guliyeva said the day had been quite hard for her. Despite the difficulties, she said such moments are a good reason to appreciate life and be grateful, and she thanked her family, friends, doctors and colleagues for their support.
