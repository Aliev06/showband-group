Beyoncé becomes the first artist with 100 Grammy nominations

18 November 20261 min read👁 viewsShowband Group
Showbiz

Famous American singer Beyoncé has reached a stunning milestone and set a new record.

A historic record and a new nomination

Nominees for the prestigious music award, to be held in February, have been announced. Beyoncé has become the first artist to reach one hundred nominations in her career — this time thanks to “Morning Dew (Donk)”.

Her rivals in this category include well-known artists Kehlani, Victoria Monét, Jill Scott and Tems.

Other stars’ successes

Another highlight is British singer Raye, who earned eleven nominations this year — the top result among female artists. Last year Beyoncé herself had the same number of nominations.
