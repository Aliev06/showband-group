Emrah İpek’s huge fortune and apartments spark debate

4 November 20261 min read👁 viewsShowband Group
Showbiz

Turkish singer Emrah İpek, famous since childhood, is in the news this time for his huge property portfolio and fortune.

Hundreds of apartments and monthly income

According to the Turkish press, the veteran artist owns hundreds of apartments in Istanbul and Bodrum. Some sources say over 200, others over 300. His monthly rental income is claimed to reach about 300,000 dollars, or 10 million lira.

A sports complex and a growing portfolio

Emrah reportedly also bought a sports complex in Istanbul for about 3 million dollars and added 12 shops and a duplex apartment in the same building to his portfolio. He is also said to own an apartment in Şişli and a villa in Bodrum worth about 8 million lira. Other claims say he once sold around 300 apartments to move into bigger projects.

No official confirmation

There is no official, confirmed information on the exact number of properties or his monthly income. His earnings were also discussed during a 2022 court case, when his monthly income was said to be about 400,000 lira.
