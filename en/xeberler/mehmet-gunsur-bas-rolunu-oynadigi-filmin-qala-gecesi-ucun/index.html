Mehmet Günsür is coming to Baku for the premiere of the film starring him

26 November 20261 min read👁 viewsShowband Group
Showbiz

It is reported that the Azerbaijani fans of beloved actor Mehmet Günsür are in for an unforgettable day as he visits our capital for the premiere of his new film.

Anticipated gala night in our capital

A special video message addressed by famous actor Mehmet Günsür to his Azerbaijani viewers on social networks has sparked great interest. In the message, the actor announced that he will visit Baku and meet his fans in person. The main purpose of the visit is to attend the premiere of the cinematic work in which he stars.

It is noted that the event will take place on November 27 at 19:00 at the CineMastercard cinema located in Deniz Mall, and is eagerly awaited by movie enthusiasts. In the film, Mehmet Günsür brings the character Özgür to life, while his co-star Devrim Özkan portrays Mavi.

A meaningful message and organ donation

It is reported that the main theme of the film revolves around organ transplantation and its vital importance in human life. Following the shooting, the creative team of the film took a step that will serve as an example to the public. Lead actors Mehmet Günsür and Devrim Özkan announced that they have pledged to donate their organs in real life as well.
